Yarnbeard

An audiobook player for your Google Drive.

Yarnbeard plays the audiobooks and audio files you already keep in your own Google Drive, and remembers exactly where you left off in every book — along with your bookmarks and notes. It runs entirely in your web browser; there is no separate account and no server storing your data.

Sign in with your Google account to open your Drive and choose which folders to add. Yarnbeard reads only the folders you point it at, so it can play the audio inside them, and saves your progress and notes to a private area of your own Drive.
